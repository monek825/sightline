Getting home without GPS

Mars has no GPS. Sightline shows whether an astronaut on a spacewalk in Jezero crater can still find the way back. It uses NASA terrain, dust and radiation data. The 200-minute walk plays at 2 EVA minutes per second.

  • Red: no landmark in sight
  • Ring: how far the astronaut can see through dust
