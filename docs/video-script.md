# 30-second video (text-to-speech voice)

Rules: ONE video link, **≤ 30 s**, English subtitles burned in, no NASA logos, nobody under 18 on screen or in the voice (that's why the voice is TTS), any AI-generated picture carries a visible "SI-generated" mark. This plan uses screen recordings and text cards only, so no AI pictures.

**Angle:** a judge thinks "Mars map = another route planner". After 30 s they should think "this is about not getting lost when you can't see home and have no GPS, and it's backed by a real Apollo failure".

**Structure:** a true story (Apollo 14) → the Mars version of the problem → three things only Sightline does → who it's for → a two-word tag.

## Voiceover (paste into ElevenLabs as one block)

In 1971, two Apollo astronauts walked toward Cone Crater.

They got within about twenty metres of the rim, and turned back. Every ridge looked like the last one.

On Mars there's no GPS, and dust hides the hills.

Sightline shows where you lose sight of home,

fixes your position from the shape of the hills,

and tells you when it's time to turn back.

For the first crews at Jezero.

Sightline. Get home.

(72 words, about 29 s at a calm 150 words per minute. ElevenLabs: a calm, low narrator voice, stability about 50, style about 20, speed 1.0. If it runs over 30 s, cut "about" and "For the first crews at Jezero." first.)
